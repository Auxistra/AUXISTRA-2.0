🎮 HOW TO USE:

1. App opens in Chrome
2. Click "Demo Mode (Skip Auth)" button
3. Click any song card (see 6 songs)
4. Song detail page opens
5. Click ⚙️ GEAR ICON (top-right corner)
6. See 4 volume sliders:
   - 🎤 Vocals
   - 🥁 Drums
   - 🎸 Bass
   - 🎹 Synths
7. Drag sliders to mix!
8. Click gear icon again to hide remix
